Return & Refund Policy

At WOW DROPS FOOD AND BEVERAGES, we are committed to delivering quality products and ensuring a smooth shopping experience. Please read our Return & Refund Policy carefully before placing an order.

1. No Return, No Exchange & No Replacement

All products sold by WOW DROPS FOOD AND BEVERAGES are non-returnable, non-exchangeable, and non-replaceable.

Once an order has been delivered, we do not accept requests for returns, exchanges, or replacements.

2. Refund Policy

Refunds are not available except in the following circumstance:

  • Duplicate payment or duplicate transaction made for the same order.

To be eligible, the refund request must be raised within 6 hours of the duplicate transaction.

3. How to Request a Refund

Customers must contact our support team with the following details:

  • Order ID
  • Transaction ID(s)
  • Proof of payment
  • Contact details

Requests can be sent to:
Email: wowdropsfoodandbeverages@gmail.com
Phone: +91 70928 88999

4. Refund Timeline

Once the duplicate transaction is verified and approved, the refund will be processed and credited within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. The actual credit timeline may vary depending on your bank or payment service provider.

5. Cancellation Policy

Orders placed cannot be cancelled.
