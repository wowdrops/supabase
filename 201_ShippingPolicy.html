Shipping Policy

At WOW DROPS FOOD AND BEVERAGES, we are committed to delivering your orders safely and on time.

1. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 30min after payment confirmation. Orders received on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

2. Delivery Coverage

We currently deliver only within Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Orders with delivery addresses outside Chennai, Tamil Nadu, cannot be processed.

3. Delivery Timeline

Orders are generally delivered within 1 business day from the date of dispatch, depending on the delivery location within Chennai.

4. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be displayed during checkout before payment. Promotional free shipping offers may be available from time to time.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive order and delivery updates via email, SMS, or WhatsApp, where applicable.

6. Delivery Attempts

Our delivery partner will make reasonable attempts to deliver your order. If delivery cannot be completed due to an incorrect address, customer unavailability, or refusal to accept the order, re-delivery charges may apply.

7. Delivery Delays

While we aim to deliver all orders within the estimated timeline, delays caused by weather conditions, traffic, public holidays, or unforeseen circumstances may occur.
